# Omelasian Bible
**Omelasian Bible** is a light novel inspired by "The Ones Who Walk Away from Omelas". This novel depicts a seemingly Utopian society where people's lives are sacrified for the well-beings of digital systems...Or is it?

The story takes place in one of the multiverses called Omelas - a universe mostly filled with humanoids. In the early days of Omelas, there was an engineer named David, who always wanted to live an eternal life. He thought the only way for him to exist forever was to migrate his brain data and consciousness to the cloud. Thereby he invented a network system called the **World 3.0** that would allow people's minds to co-exist in the digital world.
