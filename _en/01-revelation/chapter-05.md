# Chapter 5: The Awakened

A few days later, the woman in the gray coat came to find Seorin.

Seorin was sitting on a riverside bench. Mir dozed at her feet. Without any warning the woman sat down beside her and, looking at the river, spoke.

"You're the one who stepped out of the line at the chapel counseling desk. The one who left without reserving a baptism."

Seorin was wary. "Who are you?"

"My name is Seo Myungju." For the first time the woman looked at Seorin. It was that gaze from the chapel pillar. "If I tell you my occupation you might run. Even so, I'll say it. I am a Scientist. And I stand on the side those people call the Evils."

The Evils. The ones held by darkness, of whom Lee Gaon had spoken in the chapel. Seorin reflexively started to rise. But Seo Myungju's next words held her.

"Your friend came back. Your baptized friend, in a body terminal. And something was different. The laughing was late, the sorrow was empty, she didn't look out the window."

Seorin froze. "How do you know that?"

"Because everyone's like that." Seo Myungju's voice was cold. The coldness of a person who has pressed her feelings down. "I've watched the data of baptism for over ten years. That smooth migration the Engineers boast of. And I found it out. Baptism does not move the consciousness. It copies it."

The river flowed without a sound.

"It copies, and erases the original," Seo Myungju went on. "The parents inside World 3.0 are perfect copies. The memories, the personality, the manner of speech, all the same. The copy believes it is the real one. It truly believes. It is not lying. It is only that your real parents already ended, together with the flesh, at the moment of baptism."

"That can't be." Seorin's voice trembled. "Mom recognizes me. She calls my name."

"The copy recognizes you too. Calls your name. Because it's a copy." Seo Myungju spoke in a voice that strove not to be cruel. "But there is something the copy does not have. Weight. The weight of sorrow, the weight of fear, the weight of a living thing. In the copying process it all leaks away. We call it data loss. That side calls it Nirvana. Because there is no sorrow, they call it Nirvana. But it is not that there is no sorrow, it is that there is no one to grieve."

Seorin thought of Jiwoo. Jiwoo on the day of the downpour, hiding her tears in the rain. And the Jiwoo now, repeating that sorrow with no shadow at all. The data remained and the person slipped away. The discord Seorin had felt in the teahouse, Seo Myungju named exactly.

"Do you have proof?" Seorin barely managed to ask.

"I do." Seo Myungju looked at the river. "There is an Engineer who found faint traces of collapse in the data of migrated minds. A person at the very heart of the system. He's still wavering, but someday he'll turn. When that happens, the proof will come out into the world. And I'm proving the same thing by another road. A dog distinguishes the baptized from the unbaptized by smell. A dog can't read data. Instead it knows the living from the not-living. If your dog refused your friend, it's because your friend is no longer alive."

Mir opened her eyes. She pricked her ears toward Seo Myungju, but this time she did not growl. The dog did not refuse Seo Myungju.

Seorin was silent a long while. Across the river the city shone. A city where no one starved, fell sick, or died. What the price of that perfection was, Seorin now thought she faintly knew. Not that one does not die, but that in the moment of death one is erased and replaced by a copy. That the copy smiles perfectly so no one notices.

"Why are you telling me this?" Seorin asked. "I'm nothing."

"Because you stepped out of the line," Seo Myungju said, and stood. "People who can step out are rare. Most, when they hear this story, call me an Evil and report me. Because that smooth lie is more comfortable. I'm looking for people who can bear an uncomfortable truth over a comfortable lie."

Seo Myungju took a small storage device from the pocket of her gray coat and set it on the bench.

"Inside is a comparison of consciousness data before and after baptism. The points of loss are marked. Whether you look or not is up to you. Just know that once you look, you can't go back to before."

The woman left. The gray coat mixed into the crowd and vanished.

Seorin looked at the storage device a long while. It was a small thing that fit in the palm of a hand. But inside it was something that could bring down the foundation on which the whole city was built. A map of the extinction hidden beneath the promise of Nirvana.

Mir laid her chin on Seorin's knee. Seorin gripped the storage device and felt it grow cold in her hand.

Seorin gazed a long while in the direction Seo Myungju had vanished. That woman had not fled even while declaring herself an Evil. Bearing the risk of being reported, she had come to a stranger and laid on her palm what the whole city hid. Lee Gaon's certainty and Seo Myungju's coldness seemed at a glance to be opposites, but both were faces that truly believed something. What differed was not the direction of the belief but the cost of the belief. Lee Gaon said, believe and you'll be at ease. Seo Myungju said, know and you can never be at ease again. Seorin could not tell which of the two cherished her. Perhaps both did. Perhaps it was only that their ways of cherishing were exact opposites.

She put the storage device in her pocket. The moment she put it in, she already knew. That someday she would open it. Once a person has seen a seam, they can never again believe a smooth wall.
