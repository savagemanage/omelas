# Omelas: Revelation

> Book One of the Omelasian Bible. It follows the terms, characters, chronology, and synopsis 4.1 of the canonical story bible (canon/story-bible-ko.md).

## Synopsis

In Year Zero, Omelas stands as a perfect Mechanical Utopia. World 3.0 is its backbone and its pride, and people believe that through baptism they shed the flesh and reach Nirvana. On the day the Evangelist Lee Gaon cries repentance and baptism to the Untransformed in the middle of a Gangnam street, the Untransformed young woman Han Seorin witnesses the sermon and steps into this story. She had longed for baptism, but she senses a subtle "difference" in an acquaintance who has been baptized and returned, and a brief encounter with the Evils' Scientist Seo Myungju opens the first crack of doubt. Beneath the radiant surface of paradise, something is quietly collapsing. The book closes on an apocalyptic hook that hints at the hidden price of the utopia, and opens the door to the entire series.
