# Omelas: Judges

> Book Four of the Omelasian Bible. It follows the terms, characters, chronology, and synopsis 4.4 of the canonical story bible (canon/story-bible-ko.md).

## Synopsis

The ones who left were not forgotten. Of the five who had been planting seeds in the darkness beyond the lattice, a few are caught again and brought back into the city. Omelas does not execute them. The city does not use the old word execution. Instead it stands them before a tribunal. In the name of love, in the name of order, and above all in the name of saving them.

But it was not only the resistance who came before the tribunal. The moment the Scientist Seo Myungju tries to prove with data, from the witness stand, that baptism erases the original and seats a copy in its place, the thing on trial becomes baptism itself, and World 3.0 itself. Clumsy Scientists rise to defend it. They too are sincere. Noa, the robot-Good, from the seat of the counter-judge, says that robots ought to become human, and shakes the very ground of judgment. The doubting Engineer Jung Dohyun, in the gallery, the loss records of the pipeline he designed tucked in his pocket, is collapsing over whether to testify or to keep silent.

This book sets one question at the very center of the courtroom. Who holds the right to judge whom. Can the one who erases judge the one who is erased, can the one who believes he saves judge the one who refuses salvation. A verdict is handed down, but whether that verdict is justice, no one knows.
