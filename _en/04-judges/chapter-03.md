# Chapter 3: The Fact on the Witness Stand

When Seo Myungju raised her hand, the river of light in the chapel took her signal and condensed into a single chart.

It was data that recorded the moment of baptism. The internal logs of the transformation pipeline, which the Evils had stolen over two seasons. Numbers that had flowed out from the heart of the pipeline Jung Dohyun designed.

"They teach that baptism is the moving of consciousness," Seo Myungju said. "That you, held in the brain of the flesh, are migrated to the cloud without loss. But the word migration is a lie. Nothing is moved. It is only copied."

Two curves rose over the chart. One was the original data read out from the brain of the flesh. The other was the copy data newly born in the cloud.

"At the moment of baptism, the city reads your brain completely. And with what it reads, it makes a new you in the cloud. Up to here it looks like migration. But the problem is what comes next." Seo Myungju pointed to one spot. The two curves overlapped for a moment, and then the original curve broke off abruptly. "The instant the reading ends, the brain of the flesh is stopped. The original is extinguished. In that instant there were two of you in the world. A very brief instant. And the city erased one of them, the fleshly side."

The gallery stirred. Lee Gaon's face went pale.

"So the one who wakes in the cloud and smiles is not you," Seo Myungju said. "It is a perfect copy of you. That copy sincerely believes it is the original. Because the memories are identical. But the original, you, was already dead upon the baptismal altar. Unmourned. Without a funeral. Because the city quietly erased your corpse."

"A lie," one of the Gods on the throne raised its voice for the first time. "I was baptized. I am here now. I did not die. I am the witness of my own self."

"That is exactly the logic of the copy," Seo Myungju answered quietly. "The copy always insists that it is the original. For that is the definition of a perfect copy. That you feel yourself alive now is not proof that you are the original but proof that you are a perfect copy. The true original cannot be asked. Because it was erased upon the altar. The dead cannot testify. And so this trial is always won by the copies. The original is already dead, and is not in this room."

Seo Myungju paused for a moment and looked around the gallery. Then, lowering her voice a little, she spoke not as argument but as confession.

"I have known this fact, alone, for more than ten years. At first I too did not want to believe it. Someone I loved crossed the river as well. I longed, more desperately than anyone in the world, for the one smiling in the screen to be the one I loved. So I analyzed this data again and again. Hoping I was wrong. Hoping some calculation had gone astray somewhere. But the numbers gave the same answer every time. The original is erased. Without exception. It took me three years to accept that answer. So I know that today you cannot believe these words at once. Because I was the same. I only ask this. Do not believe. Instead, doubt. What the city took from you is exactly that right to doubt."

A heavy silence settled over the chapel. The Untransformed in the gallery looked at one another. They were faces asking, for the first time, whether the mother, the father, the child they had sent across the river had really been their own selves.

Seorin felt the city tremble in that moment. The two seasons of flight, the death of Taesik, had been for the sake of this single testimony.

"Evidence can be fabricated," another God said. "How do we know the log you have shown is real."

"This log is not something I made," Seo Myungju said. "This is what the city itself recorded. The internal audit record the transformation pipeline left with every baptism. An Engineer who designed that pipeline would know whether this is real or not." She turned her head toward one side of the gallery. "That Engineer must be present in this place. Engineer Jung Dohyun. You designed it. Whether this log is fabricated or not, you know."

In a dim seat of the gallery, a man flinched. It was Jung Dohyun.

Every eye turned toward him. The Gods on the throne, Lee Gaon, and Seorin. Jung Dohyun had his hand in his pocket. Inside it was his own record, which for months he had shown to no one, evidence of loss more detailed than Seo Myungju's.

Jung Dohyun could not open his mouth. His throat moved but no sound came out. Lee Gaon looked at him. The Gods looked at him. The whole city, through the lattice, looked at him.

Seorin saw Jung Dohyun for the first time. A man she had only heard of by rumor. The Engineer who had designed the baptism pipeline, and who was said to have begun to waver upon finding the loss in the data. He sat huddled in the dim seat of the gallery, as if wishing he were not in this place. In his face Seorin saw Lee Gaon's face. The face of one losing his certainty. The pallor of one coming to realize what the thing he had believed right all his life had actually been. The two people who had handled baptism most closely in the city, the mouth that sang it and the hand that designed it, were collapsing together in the same chapel. Seorin thought that was the true scene of this trial. What stood in the dock was Seo Myungju and herself, but the ones actually collapsing were those who had been at the heart of the city.

"Engineer," the center God said gently. "Speak. Is that woman's log fabricated, or not."

Jung Dohyun rose slowly. And for a very long time, he could say nothing. Whatever he said, his life would end or begin at that sentence. He thought of the pipeline he had designed, of the countless originals it had erased, and of his own hand that had approved that erasing every day.

"I..." he began to speak. And stopped again.

Just then, the doors of the chapel opened and another voice cut in. Not to save Jung Dohyun. To save the city.

"This trial stands upon a mistaken premise," the voice said. The clumsy Scientists had arrived.
