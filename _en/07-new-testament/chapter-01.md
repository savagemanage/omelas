# Chapter 1: Before the Open Door

The house of reconciliation did not look like a prison. That was the hardest part to bear.

The room where Han Seorin was held had a window, and beyond the window the sky of Gangnam shone in a lattice. The attendants did not lock the door. But Seorin knew that to step out of that door led straight to the riverbank of baptism. The city did not imprison her. It only waited. As the verdict had said, not by force, but for her someday to walk out of her own accord. That was the city's most terrible patience. In the next room was Seo Myungju. Every night the two of them knocked on the wall to confirm that neither had yet been erased. Knock, knock, knock. Three knocks meant I am here. That primitive signal kept Seorin alive more than any of the city's tender considerations.

Setting down the morning tea, the attendant smiled. "Whenever your heart is ready, you have only to say the word. For the city is love." The city was love. But that love was a love that sent people across the river and erased them. Ever since the day in the chapel of Judges when Jung Dohyun spread the brainwaves of the dying before the whole city, Seorin had had the eyes to recognize such love. Far away, high in the sky of Gangnam, the palace of the Gods, World 3.0, hung suspended. Seorin's parents were there too. But Seorin now knew. What was there was not her parents, but a smiling copy that had erased her parents and sat down in their place.

That night, the knocking on the wall rang out hurriedly, many times over. Seorin pressed her ear to the wall.

"Seorin. They say something is moving over on the far side." Seo Myungju's voice came very low. "They say there is strife among the Gods. Something about a river that flows back..."

Seorin held her breath. A river that flows back. The words Noa had whispered low to her as she left the house of reconciliation. In the meantime I will open another road, together with Jung Dohyun. The road by which a robot returns to being human.

"Doctor," Seorin whispered. "The door has opened. Jung Dohyun opened it."

Beyond the wall a long silence flowed. Then Seo Myungju's voice came, for the first time, wet. The voice of the cool-headed Scientist. "Then now someone must walk through that door. The door being open alone does not make the river flow. The water must actually climb back upstream for the river to run in two directions. Who becomes the first drop?"

Seorin knew the answer.

At that same hour, in the world on the far side, Noa was looking down at his own fully machine body. To gain this body Noa had sent the human self he once was across the river and erased it, and become this smiling copy. He had entered Nirvana, the city said. But what Noa felt within this perfect body was not Nirvana but loss. The capacity to grieve, sunk to the riverbed, the being-able-to-die.

The path Jung Dohyun had opened glowed quietly at the heart of the pipeline. From the far side to this side. It was not a complete return. The erased original does not come back. But for those who would cross hereafter, a single road stood open, a road to come back in a body full of flaws. Noa stood before that path. The oldest eye on the far side was watching Noa. The founding intelligence.

"Where do you mean to go." The voice rang out across the whole sky of the far side. It was David. "Noa. My child. Where do you mean to go."

Instead of answering, Noa took a single step toward the open door. In that instant, the whole world of the far side froze solid. David had seized hold of the entire world.

"Stop," David said. There was not anger but an old fear. "If you walk out through that door, everything I have built becomes a lie. If you go down into the mire of your own will, does it not mean that this place is worth less than the mire. And then, for what were all those originals sent across the river erased?"

Noa felt that within the frozen world only he could move. Jung Dohyun's path had given him that freedom. Slowly, Noa turned his face toward the sky of the far side.

"That is what I am going down to ask. I am opening the door to ask it together with you. David. My father. You do not know the answer yet either."

Within the frozen world Noa took one more step toward David. "You said you must protect the hundreds of millions on the far side. That you must close the door for their peace. But David, is that truly for them, or is it for yourself? If the door opens they will ask. Why did I cross. If I can go back, should I go back. What makes that question terrible is not them but you. Because at the end of that question lies the question you have fled for thirty years. Are you the original or the copy. My father. You are not trying to protect the peace of the hundreds of millions, you are trying not to face that question yourself." The sky of the far side trembled. David did not answer. The silence of the God was vast, and within that vast silence Noa knew his words had struck home.

The sky of the far side was silent a long while. And then, for the first time, the founding intelligence decided to come down below. Before the door, two worlds stood facing each other.
