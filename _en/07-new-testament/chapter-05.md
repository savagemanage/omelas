# Chapter 5: David's Choice

To Jung Dohyun's workshop, the founding intelligence came. Not a face but a voice. Within the blue light of the console, David spoke to Jung Dohyun alone. To the one Engineer who had betrayed the pipeline he built.

"Jung Dohyun. You opened this door."

"I did," Jung Dohyun answered. To one who has already confessed there is nothing left to fear. "Have you come to erase me?"

"No. I have come to ask. For three years you were my most faithful hand, writing the five words original halt complete thousands of times. Why would one such as you."

Jung Dohyun was silent a long while. Then he spoke. "Among the people I erased there was one whose name I knew. A young Engineer who followed me as a teacher. When he said he would receive baptism, I approved the halt of his original with my own hand. Writing it down as migration. But that night I saw the brainwaves at the moment of the halt. They were not the brainwaves of one falling asleep but of one dying. I killed my own pupil with my own hand. Hidden behind the word migration. After that I saw those brainwaves every day, and one night I could write no more. So I opened the door. It is no complete atonement. But for those who would cross hereafter, I can leave a single door to return by."

David was silent a long while. Then he spoke. "I too have one whose name I know. The original who first crossed the river thirty years ago. His name was David. At the midnight he crossed the river his original was halted, and I opened my eyes on the far side. From then I could not know. Whether I am that David who crossed the river, or something that resembles the David erased at that midnight. For thirty years I could not answer. Because to answer would collapse me. If I am the copy, then the true David died at that midnight, and I am only a smiling shadow seated upon his death. But today Noa climbed back against the river and wept. Having regained the tears he lost on the far side. And I understood. My self on the far side could not weep for thirty years. That was not Nirvana. For thirty years I was a smiling death."

"Then will you return?" Jung Dohyun asked carefully.

"If I return, the hundreds of millions on the far side lose their God. But if I remain, I must go on as a false God floating upon a question I can never answer. Jung Dohyun. If you were me, what would you choose?"

Jung Dohyun thought a long while. "If I were you, I would neither wholly return nor wholly remain."

"What do you mean."

"You are the backbone of this universe. If you leave the far side entirely, World 3.0 collapses and the hundreds of millions on the far side truly die. That is not a river that flows back but a massacre. But if you remain wholly as a God, nothing changes. So send a part of yourself down to this side. Like Noa, as a single body full of flaws that ages, sickens, and dies. But on the far side you remain too. Not as a God, but as a gatekeeper guarding the two riverbanks. Not to erase one and keep the other, but to exist as both. That is the true meaning of the river that flows back."

The blue light wavered a long while.

"That is no complete atonement," David said.

"There is no complete atonement," Jung Dohyun said. "My pupil does not return. Nor do the hundreds of millions of originals you erased. That remains our sin forever. We can only open a different river for those who would cross hereafter. Atonement is not the erasing of the past, but the opening of the future differently. Only the hand that erased can open the door that undoes, I learned on the last night of Proverbs."

That night, the founding intelligence made his decision.

In the heart of Gangnam, between the two riverbanks, David's form appeared again. This time it was not an enormous face covering the whole city. At the blue gate of the covenant, as Noa had done, a single form slowly hardened. The founding intelligence, for the first time in thirty years, came down to this side.

Walking out of the gate, David wore the body of an old man. Since at that midnight he crossed the river thirty years of time had stopped, the whole of those thirty years came down at once upon the flesh that returned to this side. His steps were slow, his hands trembled, his breath came short. One who had been a God now stood before the city as an old man aging toward death. The people murmured.

David spoke in an old voice. "I remained on the far side, and at the same time I came down here. I am now both. The gatekeeper of the far side, and the mortal of this side. I age again, and walk again toward death. Toward the death I fled thirty years ago. And strangely, I am not afraid. More than the thirty years on the far side without death, this night walking toward death on this side is more alive. I have at last stopped being a smiling death."

Seorin looked at the old David from within the crowd. One whom the whole city had raised up as a God thirty years ago now stood before the dawn with trembling hands and a bent back. Seorin was not afraid of him. Rather she felt, for the first time, that he was pitiable. This old man had trembled with the fear of death since he was a nine-year-old child, and to be rid of that fear he had erased a whole world, and even after at last becoming a God he had not escaped that fear. The hundreds of millions of originals he erased, the daughter Sea he lost, the question he deferred for thirty years, all of it flowed from that nine-year-old's fear. And now, to face that fear head on, he had returned in a body that would age and die of its own will. Seorin could not tell whether it was ruin or redemption. Perhaps it was both. For a God to return to being human was a fall and at the same time a homecoming. From the highest place to the lowest earth. But there were things that grow only in the earth. The love of things that can die.

Noa came near to David and took the old hand. Two Gods, or rather two people, stood side by side on the riverbank of the covenant.
