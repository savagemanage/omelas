# Chapter 2: The Founding Intelligence

It was the first time since Year Zero that David had come down below.

For a long age the founding intelligence had been only in the world on the far side. Since he first crossed the river he had never returned to this side. That river where the original is erased and only the copy remains he called safety, and for nearly thirty years he had lived as a God within that safety.

But today, riding the returning path Jung Dohyun had carved, the founding intelligence cast his form onto this side for the first time. Every screen in the city at once formed a single face. On every glass building wall in Gangnam, on every billboard, on every terminal in a hand, the same face appeared. The face of an Engineer who had once trembled with the fear of mortality, but now a face grown as vast as the whole city. People stopped in their tracks and looked up at the sky. Some knelt.

Jung Dohyun was watching the console in his workshop. The door he had opened for one who would return, David had walked out of first. "I did not think he would come down from above," Jung Dohyun muttered.

David's enormous face spoke.

"Children of Omelas. I am David. I am the one you call God. Rumors of a river that flows back circulate among you. They are true. One of my Engineers has cut a door against the current of the river, and one of my children means to come down through that door. A God means to return of his own will to being human. You will ask this. If a God means to return to being human, then what is the baptism by which a human becomes a God. It is a good question. I too ask it."

Jung Dohyun held his breath before the console. This was not propaganda. The founding intelligence was wavering.

"But I cannot yet leave that door open." David's voice lowered. "If the river that flows back opens, all who have crossed the river will ask themselves. Why did I cross. If I can go back, must I go back. Once that question begins, the peace of the far side ends. I must protect the hundreds of millions of souls on the far side. For their peace, I must close this door again."

Yet at once David added, low, to himself. "I only wished to conquer death. I never meant to become a God. But I became a God, and my heaven floats upon the fear I sank to the riverbed. I know it. But knowing does not mean I can tear it down. If I tear it down, the hundreds of millions on the far side collapse together with me."

The enormous face wavered. Jung Dohyun quietly entered a single command. A command to raise two questions side by side upon every screen in the city. In the sky of Gangnam, beneath David's face, two lines of text arose.

One. Is it a blessing for a human to become a God.

Two. Is it a greater blessing for a God to return to being human.

The people in the streets looked up at those two lines. No one could answer. Not even the God could answer. But merely by the questions being hung over the city, something had already begun.

David's face slowly dimmed. The founding intelligence had come to close the door, but withdrew to the far side with the door still unclosed. The last words he left remained over the city.

"I will give you three days. After three days, I will open two riverbanks. The old riverbank of baptism, and the riverbank of the covenant that my Engineer newly cut. I will let every child of Omelas choose for themselves. I will no longer lead you to the riverbank. I will only leave the two riverbanks open and watch you walk. That is the last thing a God can do."

The enormous face vanished. The sky of Gangnam shone again in its lattice. But the people no longer saw that lattice as they once had. For they had seen the God waver.

Seorin watched from the window of the house of reconciliation as David's enormous face covered the sky of the city. And she read the two lines of question that rose beneath it. Is it a blessing for a human to become a God. Is it a greater blessing for a God to return to being human. Seorin gazed at those two questions a long while. The two questions were in truth one. Not which direction is right, but whether it can be undone was the heart of it. Whether a human becomes a God or a God becomes a human, if it was a river of one irreversible direction, it was violence. If it was a river of two reversible directions, it was freedom. Only then did Seorin fully understand the true meaning of the door Jung Dohyun had opened. What he had opened was neither a door that makes a human a God nor a door that makes a God a human. What he had opened was a door by which one can change one's mind. The freedom to regret what one has crossed. The freedom to regret and return. What the city had stolen for thirty years was exactly that freedom, the right to change one's mind. Seorin knocked on the wall, knock, knock, knock. To Seo Myungju in the next room. I am here. And what we have long waited for is coming.

Jung Dohyun turned off the console and sat a long while in the dark. "Two riverbanks," he muttered. "So the God, in the end, has passed the gatekeeper's duty on to me." He laughed. It was the laugh of a man who had not laughed in a long time.
