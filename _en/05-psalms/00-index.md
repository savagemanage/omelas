# Omelas: Psalms

## Synopsis

After the trial was over, the city grew beautiful again. The Gangnam sky still shone with the lattice, and the river still flowed one way. Because what collapsed in the chapel of Judges collapsed soundlessly, not a single crack showed on the city's surface. But inside the people, something went on trickling down.

There are almost no events in this book. Instead there are voices. Between that winter and spring, when the trial had ended nothing, it gathers the songs and prayers and laments and monologues that people crossing all the camps sang, each from their own place. How the hymn of the Evangelist who cried out baptism turns into a question, what a mother who sent her son across the river lost, to whom the Scientist who proved the truth prays, and above all what those who have already crossed the river feel, or fail to feel, inside World 3.0.

This is a catching of breath between the books of action. But even while catching its breath, the question grows. Is becoming a machine Nirvana or annihilation. Between the one who erased and the one erased, who aches longer. The song does not answer. It only endures.
