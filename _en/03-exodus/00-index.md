# Omelas: Exodus

## Synopsis

The revelation is over, and now the leaving remains. Han Seorin, the Untransformed woman who has learned that baptism does not save a person but erases the original and seats a smiling copy in its place, flees from paradise on the night the white-robed attendant presses her doorbell. She goes to the Scientist Seo Myungju and the Evils, and a resistance that had been scattered takes shape around her. Seo Myungju and the Scientists gather proof that baptism is copying followed by extinction of the original, and try to carry that proof beyond the city.

But the city does not forget the lost sheep. The Evangelist Lee Gaon, in the name of love, and the Engineers, in the name of order, pursue them. Through journey and pursuit the resistance pays a price. There is one who is caught, one who returns to the riverside of their own accord, and one who loses their life. In the end a small band walks out beyond the lattice of Omelas, into a darkness where nothing is guaranteed. They leave without fully knowing what is right. Like the people who left that city Le Guin told of long ago, carrying only the one thing, that they will not walk into that smooth smile.
