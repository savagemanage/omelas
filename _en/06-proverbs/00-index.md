# Omelas: Proverbs

## Synopsis

The song has stopped. The questions the Psalms only endured and did not answer now cross over to the place of debate. The verdict deferred baptism to someday, and Noa promised, together with Jung Dohyun, to open the road by which a robot returns to being human. To keep that promise, one must first answer the question. Is becoming a machine truly Nirvana, or is it annihilation dressed in the clothes of salvation. Is turning a robot back into a human a greater good, or one more arrogance.

There is no great event in this book. Instead there is talk shared while seated. Seorin and Seo Myungju, released with the day of baptism on their backs, gather at Jung Dohyun's old workshop on the outskirts of the city. There the doubting Engineer and the robot-God who would become human sit across from each other night after night, trade parables, split maxims, and press one another's logic. The clumsy Scientist Yeonu comes to defend baptism and joins the debate, and Lee Gaon, his faith collapsed, knocks at the door seeking an answer.

This is a book of ideas. But not a dry treatise, rather a drama of sleepless people arguing across a single lamp. Proverbs does not give one answer. It only presses the question to the end, until Jung Dohyun at last sets his foot down on one side. That single step he takes opens the covenant of the next book.
