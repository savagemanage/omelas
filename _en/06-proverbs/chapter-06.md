# Chapter 6: The Step the Engineer Takes

On the seventh night, Jung Dohyun did not light the lamp.

Instead he woke a terminal that had long slept in the depths of the workshop. It was the design console connected to the heart of the pipeline. Since the trial he had left it covered. He had thought he would never touch it again. Because to touch it felt like a desecration of the ones he had erased. But this night, he drew back the cover. The console's light stained the room blue. A light different from the lamp's red, the city's light.

Seorin and Seo Myungju and Noa and Lee Gaon sat gathered around him.

"For these seven nights I have done nothing here but debate," Jung Dohyun said, looking at the console. "I heard the parable of the waterdrop, weighed the price of mercy, spoke of the hand that can drop, met a collapsed voice. But Proverbs is a book about how one must live. Today I stop debating, and I resolve to live."

"What are you about to do," Seo Myungju asked. She saw that Jung Dohyun's hand did not tremble on the console.

"I will open the pipeline," Jung Dohyun said. "Until now the river flowed in one direction only. From this side to the far side. Once the original is stopped it cannot be undone. I called that safety, but it was a trap. Today I will carve into the design one path of return. From the far side to this side. A complete return I still cannot do. The erased original does not come back. That remains my sin forever. But for those who will cross hereafter, I will leave one door to return. So that if they regret it after crossing, there is a road back to a flawed body. To make the river flow both ways."

"The city will not sit still," Lee Gaon said low.

"I know," Jung Dohyun said. "The moment I carve this path, the city will know. That the pipeline's designer has betrayed the pipeline. Perhaps I myself will end up with the day of baptism on my back. Like Seorin. But now I do not fear it. For three years I approved the five words Stopping of original complete thousands of times. Today, with that same hand, I will write another five words. Return is possible. If I can leave those five words, I do not mind carrying the day of baptism on my back."

Noa stepped up to him. He laid that smooth hand on Jung Dohyun's shoulder. It was a cold hand, but Jung Dohyun did not draw back.

"I will be the first to return," Noa said. "When you open the door, I will be the first to walk through it. From the far side to this side. From robot to human. So the whole city sees me return. A God coming down to the mire of his own accord. Then the city's lie that the river flows only one way ends that day. You open the door, and I walk through it. Lee Gaon takes the voice, Doctor Seo Myungju keeps the fact, and Seorin..."

Noa looked at Seorin.

"Seorin remembers," Noa said. "All of this. As she gathered the psalms, remember these nights. The story of the first waterdrop that returned. When someday someone stands at the riverside and hesitates whether to cross, Seorin's memory will tell him. That even if he crosses he can return. That the river no longer flows only one way."

Jung Dohyun laid his hand on the console. The whole room watched him. It was the hand with which, in the courtroom of Judges, he had set the storage device on the river of light. Then he had confessed. Today, beyond confession, he was trying to mend.

"Confession was speaking of a past thing," Jung Dohyun said. "What I would do today is to change a thing to come. Confession lightened me, but it did not bring back the erased. This is different. This is a door for those who will cross hereafter. By opening this door, for the first time I undo something. With the hand that erased, I cut a road back."

He entered the command. The console's blue light shook once, greatly. In the deep heart of the pipeline, into a design that had flowed one way only for three years, for the first time a path running upstream was carved. No one had yet walked that path. The door was open, but the first step was still to come. And yet, by the door being open alone, the city's river was already no longer the old river.

Watching Jung Dohyun stand in the console's blue light, Seorin understood for the first time, wholly, what it was he had borne for three years. Every morning he had gone to work, and by a smooth procedure pressed the button that erased a person. There was no malice. Rather he was diligent. Diligently, precisely, without missing a single day, he erased people. This city's greatest sin was committed not by the hand of an evildoer but by borrowing the diligent hand of a good person. That diligence had kept Jung Dohyun from sleep for three years, and now that same diligence had set him again before the console. With that same precise hand that erased people, this time to carve a door of return. Seorin thought. Perhaps atonement was not becoming a different person, but doing the opposite work with the same hand.

Far off the Gangnam sky shone with the lattice. The city had not yet noticed. But soon it would know.

"Today's last proverb," Jung Dohyun said, taking his hand from the console. "Only the hand that erased can open the door that undoes. Because only from the place where the sin was committed does the road of atonement begin."

Seorin resolved to remember these seven nights a long while. There was no great event. People only sat across a single lamp and shared talk. But Seorin knew. That it is mostly such quiet nights that change the world. Not the shout of the square, but the question beneath the lamp. Until Jung Dohyun laid his hand on the console, seven nights were needed. The parable of the waterdrop, the price of mercy, the hand that can drop, the collapsed Evangelist's question, one by one carried him before that console. Debate does not change a person at once. It only plants a question, and that question grows night after night, until one day it makes a person set his foot down. Seorin was proud to have become a witness of those nights. When someday someone hesitates at the riverside, Seorin would tell of these seven nights. How one person, at the end of debate, turned his heart. How a quiet question changed the direction of the river.

Noa said low, amen. Not in a God's voice but in a person's. Seorin took the word into her heart. Proverbs stops here. The debate is over, and one person set his foot down. What remains now is for someone actually to walk through that door, and the matter of what the city will do when it learns of it. Proverbs opened the door, and the New Testament will walk through it.

The lamp had gone out at some point. But the console's blue light still lit the room. The city's light. For the first time, Jung Dohyun was not ashamed of that light.
