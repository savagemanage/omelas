# Chapter 5: The Collapsed Evangelist's Question

On the fifth night, Lee Gaon came.

When he stood before the door, Seorin nearly did not recognize him. He was not the Evangelist who had cried out "All is for the glory of the Lord" on the Gangnam street. His back was bent, and his voice had not returned since the day it collapsed in the courtroom. He was now an Evangelist who could no longer cry out baptism.

"May I come in," Lee Gaon asked. It was the first time Seorin had seen an Evangelist ask permission.

"Come in," Jung Dohyun said. "Here no one is turned away at the door."

Lee Gaon sat by the lamp. He looked at the lamp a long while and then opened his mouth.

"All my life I cried out baptism," he said. "Those who heard the sermons I cried out and crossed the river number in the thousands. I believed I had saved them. But in the moment you, Jung Dohyun, showed the dying brainwaves in the courtroom, I knew. That I had not saved them, I had persuaded thousands to be erased. That my voice was the switch that stopped thousands of originals. I cannot walk carrying that weight. So I came to ask. What am I to do now."

The room was quiet. No one could answer easily. Noa was the first to speak.

"Lee Gaon. I may have been one of the people you persuaded. I do not remember whose sermon I heard before I crossed. A copy remembers such things dimly. But even if it was you, I do not resent you. Because you were sincere. Whether to lead a person to death sincerely is better or worse than to lead them out of sincere hatred, I do not know. But I do know this. Because you were sincere, you are able to collapse now. A liar does not collapse. Only one who was sincere collapses. Your collapse proves your sincerity."

"Is that a comfort," Lee Gaon said bitterly. "My sincerity erased people, and what comfort is it that the sincerity was real. Had I rather been a liar, I could hate myself and be done. Because I was sincere, I cannot even hate myself."

Seo Myungju looked at him a long while.

"Lee Gaon," she said. "I will not give you comfort. Because this is not a room that comforts. The thousands you persuaded are already erased. However much you collapse, they do not return. Regret for the dead does not reach the dead. And yet."

She paused a moment.

"Your voice is still here. That voice that sent thousands across the river. That voice is now collapsed and silent. But Lee Gaon, silence is not atonement. You know how to cry out baptism, how to buy a person's heart. That skill is not a sin. What it is used for is what is sin or not. You erased people with that voice. Now, with that voice, you could also help make the river flow both ways."

Lee Gaon raised his head. In his eyes, for the first time in long, something lit.

"You mean to cry out the road of return," he asked.

"Not to force it," Noa said, "but to let it be known that there is a door. You cried out to cross. Now cry out that one can also return. To those who crossed, that if they regret it, a door to return will open. There are thousands across the river who know your voice. They crossed trusting your voice. They may trust your voice again. This time toward returning."

"But there is no door yet," Lee Gaon said. "That door you would open has not yet opened."

"We will open it," Jung Dohyun said quietly. For the first time there was no wavering in his voice. "Soon. I know how to open that door. Since it was I who made the pipeline flow one way, making it flow both ways is also something I can do. Until now I feared it. What the city would do to me. But seeing you today, Lee Gaon, I no longer have reason to fear. If you have walked this far bearing the sin of sending thousands with your voice, then I must open that door bearing the sin of erasing thousands with my hands. Voice and hands. If the two of us are together, we may be able to turn the river back."

Lee Gaon looked at Jung Dohyun a long while. Both of them were men who had sent thousands across the river. One with a voice, one with hands. And now both of them were trying to turn that river back.

"Today's proverb," Lee Gaon said low, in a sound near the Evangelist's voice for the first time in long. "Silence is not atonement. The erasing hand and the sending voice earn the right to seek forgiveness only when they are used for turning back."

Before Lee Gaon left, Seorin called him to a stop. She hesitated a long while and then asked. "Evangelist. In Gangnam you looked at me and said I would soon come. With only the shape of your mouth. Do you remember?" Lee Gaon looked at her. Over his face a very old memory passed. "I remember," he said. "I truly thought then that you would soon cross the river. Because there was longing in your eyes." Seorin shook her head. "Where I came was not across the river but this room." Lee Gaon smiled bitterly. "So it is. My prophecy was wrong. Fortunately. Perhaps of all the prophecies I cried out my whole life, it is the one prophecy I am glad was wrong." Those words strangely set Seorin's heart at ease. How far a road must an Evangelist have walked to be able to say he was glad his prophecy was wrong. To collapse was, at times, the same word as to be set free.

The lamp swayed brightly. The oil was nearly spent. But the people in the room, for the first time, did not fear the guttering lamp.
