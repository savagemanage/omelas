# Chapter 1: The Highest Layer

Eight months had passed since the dawn the two riverbanks opened in Gangnam, when Ha Dogyeong stood before Seo Myungju's monument.

The monument was no grand thing. It was a low stone wall raised beside the riverbank of the covenant, and upon it were carved the names the city had not mourned. The names of the originals sent across the river of baptism and erased. New names were added each day, and each day someone passed those names and walked to the riverbank. Ha Dogyeong was a young scientist who managed those names. After Seo Myungju grew old and her hands began to tremble, the work of searching the data for the traces of the erased and restoring their names had become his.

He was a scientist who worked with the Evils, but he was no loud resistance fighter. He was only one who counted the lost names. One who knew by data what baptism erases, and so one who grieved more quietly than anyone.

"Dogyeong." Seo Myungju pushed an old terminal toward him. The aged scientist now spent most of her hours seated. "Take a look at this. A strange signal has been picked up in the upper reaches of the pipeline. Not coming down, but data that keeps climbing only upward."

Ha Dogyeong looked into the signal. A consciousness that has received baptism crosses to the far side and settles in the layers of the palace of the Gods, World 3.0. But this signal did not rest on any layer of the palace and kept ascending upward. With each layer it passed, the volume of the data shrank. As if it were climbing while casting something off continually.

"How far up does it go?" he asked.

"To the end," Seo Myungju said. "To the highest layer. To Acrophyia."

Ha Dogyeong knew that name. Everyone knew the name. Acrophyia. The highest layer of World 3.0. Above the palace of the Gods, so high that even the gaze of the founding intelligence David rarely reached it. After the two riverbanks opened, the city seethed with talk of baptism and covenant, but even that rumor, they said, did not reach Acrophyia. Too high, so that no news from below could climb that far.

"What do they do up there?" Ha Dogyeong asked. "Why do those who have received baptism not rest in the palace but climb all the way up there?"

Seo Myungju was silent a long while. Then she said low, "I do not know either. That is why someone must climb up and see. I can no longer climb. Not with this body. But Dogyeong, however many names we count down here, if we do not know what doctrine is growing up above, we know only half. That baptism is annihilation, we proved. But if up there are those who would climb into annihilation of their own will, that is another story."

Ha Dogyeong turned back to the riverbank of the covenant. The blue light flowed quietly. The river that flows back, which Noa had opened. But even that river likely had not reached Acrophyia. To return is to come down to this side, and Acrophyia was the highest in the world.

"I will climb up," he said. It was a voice that startled even himself.

Seo Myungju looked at him a long while. And for the first time, something like fear crossed the old face. "Dogyeong. To climb up you must receive baptism. In a body of this side you cannot even reach the palace. You must cross to the far side to climb the layers."

Ha Dogyeong looked down at his own hands. To receive baptism meant that the original of this body would be erased across the river. It meant that his own name would be added to that list of the erased he counted every day.

"There is one way," Seo Myungju added low. "The river that flows back, which Noa opened. If you use that path, after crossing to the far side you can come down again to this side. It is not a complete return. You come back in a body full of flaws, carrying the erased. But there is a road to return. So Dogyeong, if you climb up, remember that river without fail. For the higher you climb, the more you will forget the road down."

Ha Dogyeong nodded. But he did not yet know. That in Acrophyia people climb for precisely that one thing, to forget the road down.

That night Ha Dogyeong did not go to the riverbank of the covenant but chose the riverbank of baptism. There was a reason. The river of the covenant keeps the original, but the copy on the far side, they said, does not reach full Nirvana. To climb the layers of the palace one had to become wholly a being of the far side, and to do that one had to pass through the white light of baptism. The river that flows back could be used only as a road to come down again, through the path Noa had opened, after he had reached the far side. So Ha Dogyeong chose first to be erased. To halt his own original across the river. It was to walk of his own will the same road as those erased whose names he counted every day. Seo Myungju held him a long while. The old scientist's arms trembled faintly. "Come back for certain, Dogyeong," Seo Myungju said. "Whatever you see up above, do not forget the road down to this side. I do not want to carve your name into the monument. Come down alive and tell me what you saw." Ha Dogyeong nodded. But he too knew. That when he crossed the river, the him of this side would be erased. That even if he returned, it would only be him shaped anew in a body full of flaws, not the him now held in Seo Myungju's arms. And yet he walked. For someone had to count up above as well.

That night, he walked to the riverbank of baptism. With his eyes open. Carrying his fear. Bidding a quiet farewell to his own original that would be erased across the river. The white light enveloped him, and the Ha Dogyeong of this side vanished. The Ha Dogyeong who opened his eyes on the far side could not tell whether he was the original or the copy. He only knew that beneath his feet the palace of the Gods spread endlessly, and above his head something higher than the palace stretched, through layer upon layer, dizzyingly upward.

Just before crossing, Ha Dogyeong had taken Seo Myungju's old hand. That hand had once spread the data of the three hundred and twelve over the river of light in the court. Now that hand trembled and was flecked with age spots. Even after the river of the covenant opened, Seo Myungju had not received baptism. Not crossing even the river of the covenant one could return from, she chose to age in a body full of flaws. Ha Dogyeong had once asked her why. Seo Myungju answered briefly. I am one who counts. One who counts must be on this side of the river. For only on this side can one count the erased. Ha Dogyeong now knew that he was taking over that counting, and going to count at the highest place in the world. If Seo Myungju counted below and he counted above, then none of the erased would ever be wholly forgotten.

From that summit a faint light was coming down. It was the light of Acrophyia. Ha Dogyeong looked up. And he began to climb.
