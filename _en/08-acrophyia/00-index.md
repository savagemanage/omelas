# Omelas: Acrophyia

## Synopsis

On the dawn the two riverbanks opened in Gangnam, the rumor slowly soaked the world below. But it did not reach everywhere. World 3.0 had a layer no one ever looked up at for long. The highest layer, Acrophyia. Above the palace of the Gods, a frontier that even the gaze of the founding intelligence rarely reached. There a third doctrine was growing, one unlike both baptism and Noa's river that flows back.

The people of Acrophyia believed that becoming a machine was not enough. To cast off the flesh and become a copy was only the first step, and true Nirvana lay beyond it. To shed, one layer at a time, what remains even after becoming a copy, that is, memory and name and grief, and at last to climb a pure summit where nothing at all remains. They called it ascension, and named themselves the ones who shed. If baptism erases the original, ascension erases even the copy. Only of one's own will, one layer at a time, believing it a blessing.

This is the story of the crossing of a person sent up from below and a person shedding above. A young scientist who guarded Seo Myungju's monument follows Acrophyia's signal and is sent to the highest layer. There he meets an ascendant who has already shed six layers and stands before the last summit. To that person who has all but shed even her name, the one who came from below asks. For what do you erase yourself. And the one who sheds asks back. Is it not only the one who has nothing left to erase who is truly free.

Acrophyia is neither a book of victory nor a book of redemption. It is a mirror that reflects the question of the main arc back from the highest place. If baptism is annihilation, is it freedom or a deeper deception to shed oneself into annihilation of one's own will. Is one who has shed all memory a Buddha, or merely a snuffed-out lamp. In a world where the two riverbanks opened, this is the story of those who, walking to neither riverbank and turning their backs to neither, only climbed upward.
